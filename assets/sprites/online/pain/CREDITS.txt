Pain (Tendo) battle sprites from Naruto Online.
Frames supplied by the owner (a single packed sheet, pain_sheet.webp).
The sheet was split into 202 frames (connected regions between the green
gaps, numbered in reading order as Asset_0..201) and cut out using the
sheet's own alpha channel, with the green spill removed.
Sheets packed with tools/sprites/frames_to_sheet.py
(--stand 104 --leg-mode feet --leg-band 6), numbered by Asset_N:
  idle 98-105 (ping-pong), run 88-90 (ping-pong, low dash),
  attack 47-59 (sweep, strikes, high kick), hit 116,117,116,
  ko 116,118,119,120,127,128,128 (head snapped back, falls, ends lying down),
  jutsu 168-182 (palm thrust; the cloak flares on 177-178),
  ultimate 187-201 (chakra rods: crouch, leap, slashes, dive, finish)
Frames 0-45 are a smaller-scale set and are not used.
thumb.webp is cropped from frame 100.
